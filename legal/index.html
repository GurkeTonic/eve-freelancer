New Eden Exordium FAQ

Legal notice

This is a private, non-commercial fan project for EVE Online — no goods or services are offered, and no revenue is generated. Under German law (§ 5 DDG — Digital Services Act), a formal "Impressum" (mandatory business notice) is not required, since that obligation applies only to commercial digital services typically offered for payment.

Contact: webmaster@tonicbeacon.com

Privacy policy

1. Controller

The private operator of this non-commercial website is responsible. Contact for privacy inquiries: webmaster@tonicbeacon.com.

2. Principle

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services, and does not itself collect personal data. There are no user accounts, no forms, and no newsletters.

3. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The website is served via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you load a page, GitHub technically processes connection data necessary to deliver and secure the service (in particular your IP address). Details: GitHub General Privacy Statement. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, efficient delivery of the website).

4. Fetching game data (EVE Online / CCP)

The game data shown on this site is loaded directly by your browser from interfaces operated by the game publisher CCP hf., Grandagarður 8, 101 Reykjavík, Iceland (domain: esi.evetech.net). Your browser technically transmits your IP address to CCP in doing so. Iceland is part of the EEA; European data-protection law applies. Details: CCP Privacy Policy. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (core function of the site: displaying live game data).

5. Local storage (localStorage)

The website optionally saves a small number of preferences only locally in your browser (localStorage) — kept separately for the New Eden and Exordium boards: your chosen home system, your chosen reference market, and the auto-refresh toggle. None of this data ever leaves your browser, and it can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

6. External links

The website links to external services (e.g. Warzone Companion, GitHub). Their respective operators are responsible for their content and data processing; linked pages only open after you click them.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority (Art. 77). Since this website does not itself store personal data, please also direct any requests about connection data to the providers named in sections 3 and 4.
