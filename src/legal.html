Privacy policy

1. Controller

The private operator of this non-commercial website is responsible. Contact for privacy questions: webmaster@tonicbeacon.com.

2. Principle

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, and does not itself collect personal data. There are no user accounts, no forms and no newsletters.

3. Hosting (GitHub Pages) and delivery (Cloudflare)

The website is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you load a page, GitHub processes the connection data needed to deliver and secure it, in particular your IP address. Details: GitHub General Privacy Statement. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, efficient delivery).

All requests pass through the network of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, which delivers the pages encrypted and protects them against abuse. As a processor, Cloudflare handles the connection data needed for this, in particular your IP address. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Details: Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, fast delivery).

Cloudflare inserts the script of its Web Analytics service (from static.cloudflareinsights.com) into the pages it delivers. This website's content security policy forbids loading it, so your browser does not load or run it, and no visits are counted.

4. Game data (EVE Online, CCP)

The game data on this site comes from interfaces run by the game's publisher, CCP hf., Grandagarður 8, 101 Reykjavík, Iceland (esi.evetech.net). It is fetched about every 15 minutes when the site is built and delivered as files with the site. Your browser does not contact CCP, and CCP receives no data about your visit.

5. Local storage

The website saves a few choices only in your browser (localStorage): for each board your home system, reference market, filters and sort order, plus auto refresh and light or dark mode. None of this leaves your browser, and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

6. External links

The website links to other services such as Warzone Companion and GitHub. Their operators are responsible for their content and data processing; a linked page only opens when you follow the link.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77). Since this website stores no personal data itself, please also direct questions about connection data to the providers named in section 3.
